# JotIt logo concepts

## Purple geometric lockup — approved identity

The user supplied a purple geometric emblem and dark wordmark as a style
reference. This variation uses an original white negative-space J inside a
violet folded-note silhouette beside the JotIt wordmark. Saved as
`jotit-geometric-purple-lockup.png`, generated with the built-in image generation
tool. The user selected this identity. `jotit-symbol.png` is the transparent
symbol extracted from the approved artwork, and `jotit-app-icon.png` is the
opaque 1024 px launcher master. The in-app wordmark adapts its text color to
the current appearance while retaining the purple symbol.

On Windows, `scripts/export_brand_icons.ps1` reproduces the exports from the approved
lockup without redesigning it. It removes only white background connected to
the symbol's outer boundary, preserving the enclosed white J. Exports cover
Android, iOS, macOS, Windows, browser favicon and PWA icons. Maskable PWA icons
use extra safe padding; iOS launcher files have an opaque white background.
Other concepts below are retained as earlier explorations.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a distinct new logo variation for JotIt, a creative note-taking app. The attached image is a STYLE AND LAYOUT REFERENCE ONLY: an extremely simple purple geometric emblem paired with a bold charcoal sans-serif wordmark on white. Do not reproduce the reference company's symbol or lettering. Make an original JotIt identity: at the left, one compact violet-purple geometric folded-paper emblem built from a thick angular square silhouette with a clipped lower-right corner and a clever clean white negative-space J inside. The J must have an unmistakable geometric hooked lower return, legible and balanced at app-icon sizes. The emblem should suggest both a folded note and the letter J, using very few large shapes and crisp straight edges. Use a vibrant solid violet around #7C4DFF, exceptionally clean vector-like 2D construction. At the right, the exact text "JotIt" in a confident bold contemporary geometric sans-serif, dark charcoal #343841, carefully kerned; capital J, lowercase o, lowercase t, capital I, lowercase t, rendered exactly as JotIt. The wordmark's capital height is slightly smaller than the square emblem, optically centered vertically with it, with generous clear spacing between emblem and wordmark. The composition is one polished horizontal brand lockup on a perfectly uniform pure-white background, generous white margins, wide approximately 3:1 canvas. No sparkle, no extra icon, no trademark or registered symbol, no tagline, no extra words, no device mockup, no presentation board, no gradient, shadow, bevel, grain, paper texture, glow, or 3D. This should look like final clean identity artwork for a real app, not a photo or a screenshot.
